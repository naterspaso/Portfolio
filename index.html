About Me

I’m Nathan, a mechanical engineering student at Cal Poly Pomona graduating in December 2026 who enjoys turning ideas into functional, reliable hardware. My experience spans mechanical design, FEA & structural analysis, GD&T & tolerance stackup analysis, design for manufacturing, prototyping, testing, and hands-on fabrication. As a Formula SAE Hubs and Uprights System Captain and mechanical engineering intern at Span, I’ve designed and manufactured performance-critical components, collaborated across disciplines, and improved products with cost, reliability, and manufacturability in mind. Outside of school and work, I build projects from scratch—from an electric drift kart to a bike suspension data-acquisition system—because I believe the best way to understand engineering is to build, test, and learn by doing.
